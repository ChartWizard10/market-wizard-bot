# SFC-2A — Setup-Family Admission Arbitration

## Objective

Convert the SFC-1 setup-family compiler from isolated evidence into a governed admission contract without letting a setup-family label become capital authority.

SFC-2A is deliberately a **pure arbitration contract**. It defines what family evidence may repair at the broad-universe model-admission boundary. It does not yet wire the contract into `prefilter.py`; that wiring is SFC-2B after this contract is green.

## Governing doctrine

The four locked families remain:

1. `BREAK_RETEST_CONTINUATION`
2. `VCP_BREAK_RETEST`
3. `SMA_CRADLE_CONTINUATION`
4. `GAP_FILL_REVERSAL`

The common sequence remains sovereign:

`structure/state -> location -> liquidity/event -> reaction/acceptance -> retest -> hold -> invalidation -> target`

A family label never grants `SNIPE_IT`, `STARTER`, capital, Discord routing, or a bypass around final execution gates.

### Why an admission lane is needed

The legacy generic prefilter is strongest at already-visible break/reclaim + FVG/OB + retest structures. That creates a known recall risk for legitimate family states such as:

- a VCP in the final contraction before the generic BOS/FVG stack is visible;
- an SMA cradle where the rising 20/50 value pocket is the meaningful location even if no FVG/OB exists;
- a gap-fill reversal where the gap boundary/reclaim supplies the real geometry;
- a family-specific reclaim with valid invalidation/target geometry that the generic estimator cannot express cleanly.

SFC-2A allows deterministic family evidence to repair those **model-admission** blind spots. GPT-5.6 may then inspect the candidate, while deterministic downstream gates remain sovereign.

## Never-rescue law

Family evidence must never rescue these blockers:

- bad/empty/insufficient/stale market data;
- blocked overhead;
- excessive extension;
- failed retest;
- hostile value alignment.

These are common truth/quality failures, not family-taxonomy problems.

## Conditional-rescue law

The following generic prefilter blockers may be superseded for **model admission only** when the family compiler supplies explicit equivalent evidence:

- `no_clear_structure` — family lifecycle provides the dedicated state/structure context;
- `mid_range_no_edge` — family location proves the ticker is at a decision state, not random mid-range;
- `no_clear_invalidation_estimate` — only when the family compiler supplies a real invalidation level;
- `no_target_path` — only when the family compiler supplies a real first target;
- `rr_below_threshold_estimate` — only when family-specific geometry independently meets the same configured R:R floor.

No missing level may be invented.

## Watch-ready vs admission-ready

`watch_ready` is not enough to override the generic prefilter. It remains observational.

`admission_ready` plus the configured minimum family score is required before the family lane can supersede a generic admission blind spot.

This preserves the distinction between:

- **NEAR / developing family evidence**;
- **family-ready candidate worth GPT-5.6 deep analysis**;
- **capital-ready execution**, which remains downstream.

## Ranking law

SFC-2A defines an `admission_rank_score` that may be higher than the legacy prefilter score when deterministic family evidence is strong.

Important:

- the legacy `prefilter_score` is never overwritten;
- the family rank influence is capped;
- the rank score is for candidate selection only;
- it is not a trade grade or probability;
- CAP-40 is still separate. The current maximum deep-analysis candidate count remains 30 until the dedicated 30-vs-40 study proves benefit.

## SFC-2B wiring contract

The next subphase may wire this object into `prefilter.py` only if:

1. no-family tickers preserve exact legacy behavior;
2. family admission cannot rescue any never-rescue blocker;
3. original vetoes remain visible for audit;
4. rescued vetoes are explicitly recorded rather than deleted silently;
5. `eligible_for_model` can be added while historical compatibility fields remain until the provider-neutral naming cleanup;
6. model candidate sorting can use `admission_rank_score` while preserving the 30-candidate cap;
7. GPT-5.6 receives the normalized family evidence in its prompt;
8. final tiering/ladder/seal remain unchanged and sovereign;
9. real 4H remains shadow/evidence-only;
10. universe, cadence, R:R floors, fragile-risk floor, cooldown, routing and capital contracts do not change.

## Acceptance tests

SFC-2A must prove:

- generic structure/mid-range blind spots can be repaired by a valid family object;
- family-specific invalidation/target/R:R can satisfy admission geometry only when explicit;
- no data/overhead/extension/retest/value failure is rescued;
- watch-ready alone cannot open the family lane;
- family score below the admission floor cannot open the family lane;
- rank influence is bounded;
- no family evidence is behaviorally inert;
- the admission object contains no tier/capital/routing authority.

## Capacity boundary

The user proposed moving the deep-analysis ceiling from 30 to 40. That remains the preferred **next ceiling only if CAP-40 proves incremental recall and operational headroom**.

SFC-2 must first make candidate admission intelligent enough that any later ten-call expansion is spent on the right names rather than compensating for a weak legacy ranking bias.
